Try it on Android

  1. Download one of the MOD files above to your device.
  2. In DigiStudio, open Disk Op and load the module using Android's file picker.
  3. Tap Play Sng. Check that you can hear the music and that the pattern rows advance. Tap Stop to end playback.

These steps also work for Google Play review and closed testing. No sign-in is required.

History

The late 1980s saw a group of school friends in Uppsala, Sweden, form The Space Pigs around a shared love of programming, electronics and the demoscene.

The group released Megademo in December 1989 with tracker music for the PC speaker and LPT port. Demozoo credits it as the first MS-DOS demo with tracker music.

DigiStudio was the group's first software tool. The DOS MOD player and editor mixed four sampled channels in software and could play them through the built-in PC speaker on an 8 MHz 286 without a sound card. Demozoo archives DigiStudio v1.5 from February 1993.

DigiStudio became the group's first commercial product when it was bundled with Covox audio hardware, a less widely adopted alternative to Sound Blaster.

Several members went on to form Frontline Design and port Digital Illusions' Pinball Fantasies and Pinball Illusions to PC. The author of this site runs Pinball Fantasies at full frame rate on an IBM 80286 running at 8 MHz, with a fast VGA card and the built-in PC speaker. A Sound Blaster makes it sound even better. Martin Lindell's history of DICE

DigiStudio Reborn brings the original four-channel mixer to modern systems with a new interface. It is a free hobby project.

Keys & effects

Keys

SPACE   stop if playing, else edit
ESC     stop
F5 / P  play
F8      stop
F1 / F2 octave
+/-     sample
TAB     channel
1–4     mute
Z..M / Q..P  piano
CTRL-O/S/V   file

Disk Op: load/save module + sample. SMP-ED: wave markers A/B, cut/trim/clr.

Effects (classic MOD)

0xy arpeggio      1xx porta up
2xx porta down    3xx tone porta
4xy vibrato       5xy tone+vol
9xx offset        Axy vol slide
Bxx pos jump      Cxx set volume
Dxx ptn break     Fxx speed/tempo
E0x filter        E9x retrig
ECx note cut      EDx note delay
